# In-game hints off (DESIGN.md, In-game hints): owl statues and a library book show their own messages, which hold
# the vanilla texts, and set no flag. Run after newgame_file2 on a mod patched without hints or Owl Gifts (the suite's).
# Martha's Bay owl: its message APHint4 says the statue's riddle.
warp Field 156.75 7.5 162.75
face up
interact
until textready 10
send DBG text APHint4
expect said=SECRETS\sARE\sLIKE\sWATER\sWHEN\sIT\sCOMES\sTO\sBRIDGES\. 5
dismiss 30
send DBG flag APHint4
expect FLAG.APHint4.0
# The library's shield book: its own question, then its own text in APHint33.
warp Library 12.76 0 5.74
face up
interact
until msg:Selecting 10
send DBG text CheckShieldBook
expect said="How\sto\sHandle\sYour\sShield\sLike\sa\sPro!" 5
choose 0
until textready 10
send DBG text APHint33
expect said="If\syou\shold 5
dismiss 30
send DBG flag APHint33
expect FLAG.APHint33.0
# Color Dungeon 06C, with its beak: one of the three statues that shared an entry point, now with its own (without
# Owl Gifts the patcher makes it), says their common text in APHint30.
warp Lv10ClothesDungeon 35.25 0 62.25
send DBG grant StoneBeak 1 9 0
expect OK.grant 5
goto 35.25 0 62.25
face up
interact
until textready 10
send DBG text APHint30
expect said=Try\sif\syou\sdare:\smake\severy\stile\ssquare\. 5
dismiss 30
send DBG flag APHint30
