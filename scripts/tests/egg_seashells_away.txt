# Seashells Required (DESIGN.md, Goal): on a seashell hunt with 3 required, the last seashell received away from the
# egg leaves it shut and Link free; the egg's opening plays once he walks up to the egg, and its door leads to the
# ending.
# Run after newgame_file2 (Link in Tarin's house) on a mod patched with Seashells Required 3 (and Instruments
# Required 0, as a seashell hunt's settings have). The owl in front of the egg's stairs talks when Link first stands there; FourteenClear marks that talk
# as done.
send DBG setflag FourteenClear 1
expect FLAG.FourteenClear.1 5
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
# Away from the egg the egg stays shut.
send DBG flag Egg_Performance
expect FLAG.Egg_Performance.0 5
level MarinTarinHouse
# From the foot of the egg's stairs (the sign's spot, then a tile right onto the stairs) Link walks up; near the top
# the opening takes him, and at its end it has opened the egg.
warp Field 96.75 20.25 24.75
goto 98.25 20.25 24.75
hold 0 0 32767 until !free 20
free 60
send DBG flag Egg_Performance
expect FLAG.Egg_Performance.1 5
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Ending 15
state
