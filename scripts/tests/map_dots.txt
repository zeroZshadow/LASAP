# The dungeon map's dots: run after newgame_file2 on a mod patched with Map Tracker on. In Tail Cave the dungeon map
# shows a dot per spot holding checks (15 on a make_test_patch.py mod), those of the shown floor visible, and no chest
# icons of the slot's chests. DBG mapdot prints "MAPDOT made=<dots made so far> page=<open> dots=<n> shown=<n> ...
# hover=<marker>/<dot>/<checks> ... looks=<letter per dot: i in logic, p partly, o out of logic, c done; capital:
# hidden> | text=<the hovered dot's name>". With the cursor put on a dot the hover takes it and its name; A on a dot of
# one check marks it skipped (looks c, "Skipped: ") and back. In Key Cavern A on the dot of two checks opens the check
# list, which B closes. Screenshots map_dots, map_dot_hover, map_dot_list and map_dots_floor.
warp Lv01TailCave 52.5 0 92.25
tapuntil 800 paused=1 when free 5
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect MAPDOT.made=\d+.page=1.dots=15.shown=\d+.inroom=15.rooms=\d+.chests=[1-9] 5
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/map_dots.png
# Feather Chest (dot 2): the cursor put beside it takes the hover and its name, and eases onto it with the stick
# idle (by the time the screenshot is taken).
send DBG mapdot near 2
send DBG mapdot
expect hover=2/2/1.at=.*text=Feather.Chest$ 5
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/map_dot_hover.png
send DBG mapdot
expect cursor=(-?\d+),(-?\d+).hover=2/2/1.at=\1,\2 5
tap 1 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
send DBG mapdot
expect hover=2/2/1.at=.*looks=..c.*text=Skipped:.Feather.Chest$ 5
check paused=1
tap 1 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
send DBG mapdot
expect hover=2/2/1.at=.*looks=..i.*text=Feather.Chest$ 5
tapuntil 800 paused=0 when dmap:KeyWait 5
free 10
# Key Cavern: the map opens on B1 (Link's floor) with its 6 dots of 21; up shows 1F's 15 and none of B1's, down
# B1's again. Screenshot map_dots_floor (1F).
warp Lv03KeyCavern 93.75 0 86.25
tapuntil 800 paused=1 when free 5
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect page=1.dots=21.shown=6.inroom=21.*floor=1.*looks=iiiIIIIIIIiiiIIIIIIII 5
# The basement's key and its owl statue (marker 10, dot 42): A opens the check list, B closes it, the map stays open.
send DBG mapdot cursor 10
send DBG mapdot
expect hover=10/42/2.at=.*text=2/2.North.Basement.Room.\+1$ 5
tap 1 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
send DBG maplist
expect open=1.*count=2/2 5
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/map_dot_list.png
tap 2 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
send DBG maplist
expect open=0 10
check paused=1
tap 2000 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect dots=21.shown=15.*floor=2.*looks=IIIiiiiiiiIIIiiiiiiii 5
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/map_dots_floor.png
tap 8000 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect dots=21.shown=6.*floor=1 5
tapuntil 800 paused=0 when dmap:KeyWait 5
free 10
