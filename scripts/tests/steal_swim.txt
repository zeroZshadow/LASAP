# Entrances and stealing in the water: the cave door in the Tal Tal waters (Field_03O) leads into the shop, so the
# shop's way out leads out of it, into the water. Link, with the flippers, leaves with a stolen item and swims: the
# item comes there (ap_jumps.cpp, TickStolenGoods), and the shopkeeper lets him back in. Sets the steal flags instead
# of stealing. Gives the flippers and leaves them. Run after newgame_file2 on a mod from make_test_patch.py --entrances
# '{"Field_03O": "Shop_01A", "Field_10D": "EagleTowerExit_01A_b"}'.
guard hurt
send DBG grant Flippers 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
send DBG setflag ScoopGet 0
expect FLAG.ScoopGet.0 5
# in the water south of the cave's door, swimming, which warp does not wait for
send DBG warp Field 218.25 12 28.5
expect OK.warp 5
inlevel 20
await 5 event=30
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Shop 10
loaded 20
level Shop
send DBG setflag ShopShovelSteal 1
expect FLAG.ShopShovelSteal.1 5
send DBG setflag StealSuccess 1
expect FLAG.StealSuccess.1 5
# its way out, south of Link, into the water: the stolen item's get plays while he swims
hold 0 0 -32767 until level=Field 10
inlevel 20
until msg:KeyWaiting 15
tapuntil 2 msg:Closed when textready 10
poll FLAG.StealSuccess.0 10 DBG flag StealSuccess
send DBG flag ScoopGet
expect FLAG.ScoopGet.1 5
await 8 event=30
# back in through the door: no punishment, Link stands free in the shop
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Shop 10
loaded 20
level Shop
free 15
wait 3
free 5
