# hits it until its dead event starts, and its drop pops where the hookshot would start. With the level loaded again
# the boss drops the item again and the drop stands there; Link takes it. Run after boot_file1; resets the boss's and the drop's flags. With its intro not seen (MasterStalfos4DemoClear off) the boss
