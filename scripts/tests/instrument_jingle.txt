# Each instrument's fanfare is its own song (DESIGN.md, Instrument songs): a received instrument's item-get, a find
# (DBG pickup), then Tail Cave's pedestal (Lv01TailCave_03G, 97.5 2.0 27.75) holding the Full Moon Cello, whose song
# plays alone (the music stopped before the item-get) and on through the whiteout until the warp out. Run after
# newgame_file2 with Instant Pickups off, on a mod from make_test_patch.py --items D1-instrument=full-moon-cello. An
# instrument's text closes by itself.
guard hurt
free 30
send DBG grant SurfHarp 1 -1
expect OK.grant 5
await 5 event=22
await 3 bgm=\S*BGM_INST_HARP:2
until msg:Reading 5
await 10 event=0
free 10
send DBG pickup ConchHorn
expect OK.pickup 5
await 3 bgm=\S*BGM_INST_HORN:2
await 5 popup=0
warp Lv01TailCave 97.5 1.5 29.25
at 97.5 29.25
goto 97.5 1.5 28.5
face up
interact
await 10 anim=item_get_st
await 3 bgm=\S*BGM_INST_VIOLIN:2
until msg:Reading 10
until msg:Closed 10
wait 2.5
check bgm=BGM_INST_VIOLIN:2\s
inlevel 30
level Field
