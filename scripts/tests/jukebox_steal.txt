# The Jukebox's Ballad through a stolen item's exit and straight back in (DESIGN.md, Music): the item's fanfare makes
# the ensemble give way, and it comes back while the shop loads, once the level has its rooms. Run after
# newgame_file2 on a mod patched with Instant Pickups; it gives Link the Surf Harp and the Coral Triangle (raw adds)
# and sets the steal flags instead of stealing. DBG music's log shows the yield and the resume.
guard hurt
send DBG give 53 1 -1
expect OK.give 5
send DBG give 55 1 -1
expect OK.give 5
send DBG music BGM_ENSEMBLE_INST
await 10 music=BGM_ENSEMBLE_INST\s.*layers=28
warp Field 51.75 10.5 117
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Shop 5
inlevel 20
level Shop
free 10
send DBG setflag ShopHeartSteal 1
expect FLAG.ShopHeartSteal.1 5
send DBG setflag StealSuccess 1
expect FLAG.StealSuccess.1 5
# Out through the door: the exit's event gives the heart (a find); the stick held up walks Link back in at once.
hold 0 0 -32767 until level=Field 10
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Shop 15
inlevel 30
level Shop
free 20
await 10 music=BGM_ENSEMBLE_INST zonebgm=BGM_SHOP bgm=BGM_ENSEMBLE_INST:1\s.*layers=28
send DBG music
expect MUSICLOG.*\|\syield\sBGM_FANFARE_ITEM_GET\s.*\|\sresume\s 5
send DBG music off
await 10 music=-
