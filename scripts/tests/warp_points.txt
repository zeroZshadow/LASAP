# Warp Points (DESIGN.md, Warp points): opening a warp point leaves it closed, sends no check, and its item opens
# it. Needs a mod with Warp Points on (make_test_patch.py --settings '{"Warp Points": true}') and runs after
# newgame_file2 (no warp point open; no client: `game` sends the ITEM lines).
# - The buried platform of Eastern Tal Tal Mountains (point 7, Field_02P, 234.75 18.75 21.75): Link digs it up from
#   the tile south of it; its item opens it and the game's first-opening message plays.
# - The Turtle Rock hole (point 1, Field_01B, 24.75 27 11.25), closed while two other points are open: Link lands in
#   it, the game's first-opening message plays, and Link is set down south of it (its look stays open until the
#   room reloads); with its item it opens the warp map, which B closes.
# - The Tal Tal Heights hole (point 2, Field_03M, 189.75 13.5 32.25), its item received first: Link lands in it and
#   the warp map opens.
# - The rock over Dampe's Shack's platform (point 5, Field_03H, 107.25 14.25 32.25): lifting it opens nothing.
# - Manbo's Pond (point 4): Link swims up to Manbo in his cave (MamboCave) with the Ocarina; his song opens nothing.
# Grants the Shovel (on Y), the Power Bracelet, the Ocarina and the Flippers (sandbox saves only).
# Screenshots: warp_1 the platform dug up, warp_2 its opening message, warp_3 lifted out of the closed hole,
# warp_4 the warp map, warp_5 the rock lifted, warp_6 after Manbo's song, warp_7 the warp map at Tal Tal Heights.
guard hurt
free 10
check warps=0\s
send DBG grant Shovel 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
send DBG poke a2 07
expect OK.poke 5
send DBG grant PowerBraceletLv1 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5

# The platform: dug up, its bit stays clear
warp Field 234.75 18.75 23.25
poll OK.enemies.killed 3 DBG enemies kill
face up
tap 8
free 5
wait 2
shot warp_1.png
check warps=0\s
game ITEM 1 $WARP:7 1 -1 -1 Warp: Eastern Tal Tal Mountains|
expect RECV\s1 10
check warps=80\s
until textready 10
shot warp_2.png
dismiss 20

# The hole: closed, with points 7 and 0 open (the warp map would show them), it lifts Link out
game ITEM 2 $WARP:0 1 -1 -1 Warp: Ukuku Prairie|
expect RECV\s2 10
check warps=81\s
send DBG warp Field 24.75 27 11.25
# The game's first-opening message, then the hole lifts Link out
until textready 15
dismiss 20
free 20
shot warp_3.png
check warps=81\s
at 24.75 14 1
game ITEM 3 $WARP:1 1 -1 -1 Warp: Turtle Rock|
expect RECV\s3 10
check warps=83\s
# Open, it takes Link in again and opens the warp map; B closes it and Link walks out
goto 24.75 27 12.75
hold 0 0 32767 until !free 5
wait 4
shot warp_4.png
tap 2
free 20
check level=Field\s

# The Tal Tal Heights hole, received before it is found: open
game ITEM 4 $WARP:2 1 -1 -1 Warp: Tal Tal Heights|
expect RECV\s4 10
check warps=87\s
send DBG warp Field 189.75 13.5 32.25
until !free 15
wait 4
shot warp_7.png
tap 2
free 20
check warps=87\s

# The rock over Dampe's Shack's platform: lifting it opens nothing
warp Field 107.25 14.25 33.75
poll OK.enemies.killed 3 DBG enemies kill
face up
interact
until carry=1+free 3
shot warp_5.png
check warps=87\s
face down
tap 1
until carry=0 3

# Manbo's song: his gift's check; the pond's bit stays clear. Link swims (event 30), which is not free.
send DBG grant Ocarina 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
send DBG grant Flippers 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
send DBG warp MamboCave 7.5 5 0
inlevel 20
until event=30 10
face right
tapuntil 1 textready 10
tapuntil 2 event=30 when textready 120
shot warp_6.png
check warps=87\s
state
