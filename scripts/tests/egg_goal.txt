# Instruments Required (DESIGN.md, Goal): with 3 required, the sign below the Wind Fish's egg says so, and the egg
# stays shut with two instruments and opens with a third. Run after newgame_file2 on a mod patched with Instruments
# Required 3 (default starting gear: the ocarina and Manbo's Mambo). DBG grant adds the Ballad and the instruments
# without an item-get.
# The sign one tile north: its text (Place:SBMountTamaranch) names the number. The owl in front of the stairs talks
# when Link first stands there; FourteenClear marks that talk as done.
send DBG setflag FourteenClear 1
expect FLAG.FourteenClear.1 5
warp Field 96.75 20.25 24.75
face up
interact
until textready 10
send DBG text SBMountTamaranch
expect said=.*Egg\sopens\swith\s3\sinstruments\. 5
dismiss 10
send DBG setflag FourteenClear 0
expect FLAG.FourteenClear.0 5
send DBG grant Song_WindFish 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
send DBG grant ConchHorn 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
send DBG grant SeaLilysBell 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
# The ocarina (item 0x0c) on X with the Ballad selected (song 1), as the item menu sets them: inventory bytes a3, a1.
send DBG poke a1 01
expect OK.poke 5
send DBG poke a3 0c
expect OK.poke 5
# On the stairs below the egg, inside the area where the Ballad starts its song.
warp Field 98.25 32.25 8.25
# Two instruments: the song plays, the egg stays shut and walking north stays on the mountain (a 3 s walk).
interact 4
until event=22 10
free 90
send DBG input 0 3000 0 32767
wait 4
level Field
# A third instrument opens it, and walking north enters the egg.
send DBG grant CoralTriangle 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
warp Field 98.25 32.25 8.25
interact 4
until event=22 10
free 90
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Lv09WindFishsEgg 15
state
