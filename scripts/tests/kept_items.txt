# The Trendy Game's prizes keep their vanilla item and are AP locations (DESIGN.md, Item model): the module reports
# one when the game adds its item, and does not grant the server's copy again. Run after newgame_file2 with the
# client connected to the server. DBG give adds an item as the game does (through the AddItemID hook).
# The second heart piece prize (index 25): its check comes back as an item that is not granted, so the heart pieces
# keep the one bit 25.
send DBG give 58 1 25
await 10 recv=1
check hp=2000000
# The Yoshi Doll (no index) sets its getting flag.
send DBG give 29 1 -1
await 10 recv=2
send DBG flag TradeYoshiDollGet
expect FLAG.TradeYoshiDollGet.1 5
check hp=2000000
