# Stealing and straight back in: Link comes into the shop with StealSuccess still set, as when a led shop exit takes
# him out and the stick held up walks him back in before the module's stolen-goods request. The shop's arrival entry
# EntranceIn_Tool walks him in and gives the item where the shopkeeper would kill him (patcher
# ShopRandomizer.makeStealingEventChanges). Sets the steal flags while the jump into the shop loads, so only the
# arrival entry can give the item. Run after newgame_file2.
guard hurt
send DBG setflag ScoopGet 0
expect FLAG.ScoopGet.0 5
warp Field 51.75 10.5 117
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Shop 5
send DBG setflag ShopShovelSteal 1
expect FLAG.ShopShovelSteal.1 5
send DBG setflag StealSuccess 1
expect FLAG.StealSuccess.1 5
level Shop
until textready 20
dismiss 20
poll FLAG.StealSuccess.0 10 DBG flag StealSuccess
send DBG flag ShopShovelSteal
expect FLAG.ShopShovelSteal.0 5
send DBG flag ScoopGet
expect FLAG.ScoopGet.1 5
free 20
level Shop
# no punishment after the item: Link stays free in the shop
wait 3
free 5
