# The Seashell Mansion's 5-shell present holds the placeholder of 5-seashell-reward, whose model is the sword in the
# suite's mod (suite.txt mod:). The present adds its item after its item-get (DESIGN.md, In-game text), so the model
# held up, by the item-get or by the find's popup (Instant Pickups), must be the present location's item's: STATE
# held=0 (SwordLv1), not the last found location's or the Archipelago model (71). Run after newgame_file2: the script
# gives seashells 0-5, marks the spirit's first talk seen and clears the 5-shell present's flag, which the present sets again.
# Screenshots: mansion_1 the present, mansion_2 the held model.
# Seashells 0-5 (inventory +0x88, a bit each), so the mansion's gauge reaches the 5-shell present, and the spirit's
# first talk seen (FirstSeashell, ShellMansionMaster's ShellMansionFirst sets it).
send DBG poke 88 3f
expect OK.poke 5
send DBG setflag FirstSeashell 1
expect FLAG.FirstSeashell.1 5
send DBG setflag GetSeashell10 0
expect FLAG.GetSeashell10.0 5
warp Field 158.25 9.82 102
at 158.25 102
hold 0 0 32767 until level=SeashellHouse 5
inlevel 15
free 10
# The gauge counts the shells, then the present appears.
hold 0 32767 0 until !free 5
free 40
shot mansion_1.png
goto 6 0 0
at 6 0
face right
interact
await 10 \sheld=0\s
shot mansion_2.png
# The item-get's text, none after a popup
tapuntil 2 free 30
send DBG flag GetSeashell10
expect FLAG.GetSeashell10.1 5
