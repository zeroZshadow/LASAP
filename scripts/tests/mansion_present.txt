# The Seashell Mansion's 5-shell present holds the placeholder of 5-seashell-reward, whose model is the sword in the
# suite's mod (suite.txt mod:). The present adds its item after its item-get (DESIGN.md, In-game text), so the model
# held up, by the item-get or by the find's popup (Instant Pickups), must be the present location's item's: STATE
# held=0 (SwordLv1), not the last found location's or the Archipelago model (71). Run after boot_file1: the save
# holds more than 5 seashells and took the 5-shell present, whose flag the script clears and the present sets again.
# Screenshots: mansion_1 the present, mansion_2 the held model.
send DBG setflag GetSeashell10 0
expect FLAG.GetSeashell10.0 5

inlevel 15
free 10

free 40

at 6 0
face right
interact
await 10 \sheld=0\s
shot mansion_2.png
