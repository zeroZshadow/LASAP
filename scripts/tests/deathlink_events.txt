# DeathLink deaths that arrive while Link is busy, on the deathlink.txt setup (after boot_file1 or deathlink). Each
# waits until Link is free and no item display starts or shows, then kills him; each game over ends with Retry.
# Ends with Link alive and free in Mabe. Patterns use \s for spaces.
guard off dead
# A talk with the Mabe shopkeeper (from the tile below him): a death and an item arrive. The death waits for the talk
# to end and kills Link before the item shows; the item shows after Retry.
warp Field 51.75 10.5 117
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Shop 5
inlevel 20
level Shop
free 10
goto 10.5 0 9
at 10.5 9
face up
interact
until textready 10
peer /die Finder fell during a talk.
logged DeathLink:\sFinder\sfell\sduring\sa\stalk 10 client
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1 1
expect OK.grant 5
wait 2
check health=[1-9].*event=[1-9]
tap 2 2 2 when textready
until health=0 10
until paused=1 10
check showq=1(\s|$)
tapuntil 1 paused=0 when paused=1 20
await 30 pos=[1-9]
await 30 showq=0\+1
until textready 10
dismiss 20
check health=[1-9]
# An item-get display: a death that arrives while it shows waits until it is dismissed.
warp Field 38.25 10.5 128.25
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1 1
expect OK.grant 5
await 10 showq=0\+1
until textready 10
peer /die Finder fell during an item.
logged DeathLink:\sFinder\sfell\sduring\san\sitem 10 client
wait 2
check health=[1-9]
tap 2 2 2 when textready
until health=0 10
until paused=1 10
tapuntil 1 paused=0 when paused=1 20
await 30 pos=[1-9]
until free 20
# A level load: a death sent right after a warp waits until Link is in the new level.
send DBG warp MarinTarinHouse 6.85 0 3.83
expect OK.warp 10
peer /die Finder fell during a load.
logged DeathLink:\sFinder\sfell\sduring\sa\sload 10 client
until health=0 30
level MarinTarinHouse
until paused=1 10
tapuntil 1 paused=0 when paused=1 20
await 30 pos=[1-9]
until free 20
check health=[1-9]
# None of these deaths went back.
logcount Sending\sdeath 0 client
logcount DeathLink:\sLink 0 peer
send DBG flag APDeathLink
expect FLAG\sAPDeathLink\s0 5
warp Field 38.25 10.5 128.25
