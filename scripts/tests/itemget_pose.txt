# The item-get holds the placeholder's model variants up in their item's pose (DESIGN.md, Showing received items):
# a heart piece found at an AP location at the heart piece's size, not the default pose of ids the game has none
# for. Needs the suite's mod (suite.txt), whose --models give the variants ids 72 (HeartPiece), 73 (MagnifyingLens),
# 74 (Seashell) and 75 (SwordLv1). Each check reads the held model's scale (DBG heldpose item=) once the item's text
# waits for a button; the popup (DBG popmodel) shows at the item-get's scale (popup=).
free 30
send DBG itemget 58 HeartPiece
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=1\.700\s
dismiss 15
send DBG itemget 72 HeartPiece
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=1\.700\s
dismiss 15
send DBG itemget 75 SwordLv1
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=1\.600\s
dismiss 15
send DBG itemget 74 Seashell
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=0\.900\s
dismiss 15
# The placeholder itself keeps the default pose its model is sized for
send DBG itemget 71 ArchipelagoItem
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=1\.000\s
dismiss 15
send DBG popmodel 72
poll HELDPOSE.*\spopup=1\.700\s 5 DBG heldpose
