# The item-get holds a found placeholder up as its location's item, in that item's pose (DESIGN.md, Item models): a
# heart piece found at an AP location at the heart piece's size, not the placeholder's default pose. Needs the suite's
# mod (suite.txt), whose --models show a heart piece at woods-north-cave-loose (local id 145), the sword at
# ukuku-cave-west-loose (84) and a seashell at graveyard-cave (33). DBG collect adds a location's placeholder, as its
# flows do before its item-get. Each check reads the held model's scale (DBG heldpose item=) once the item's text
# waits for a button; the popup of a find (DBG pickup) shows at the item-get's scale (popup=).
free 30
send DBG itemget 58 HeartPiece
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=1\.700\s
dismiss 15
send DBG collect 145
send DBG itemget 71 ArchipelagoItem
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=1\.700\s
dismiss 15
send DBG collect 84
send DBG itemget 71 ArchipelagoItem
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=1\.600\s
dismiss 15
send DBG collect 33
send DBG itemget 71 ArchipelagoItem
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=0\.900\s
dismiss 15
# A location without a model of its own keeps the placeholder's default pose, which its model is sized for
send DBG collect 1
send DBG itemget 71 ArchipelagoItem
until textready 10
send DBG heldpose
expect HELDPOSE.*\sitem=1\.000\s
dismiss 15
send DBG collect 145
send DBG pickup 71
expect OK.pickup 5
poll HELDPOSE.*\spopup=1\.700\s 5 DBG heldpose
