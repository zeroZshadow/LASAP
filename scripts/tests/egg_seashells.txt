# Seashells Required (DESIGN.md, Goal): on a seashell hunt with 3 required, the sign below the Wind Fish's egg says
# so; the egg stays shut with two seashells, and the third, received at the egg, plays its opening without the Ballad.
# Its door then leads to the ending. Run after newgame_file2 on a mod patched with Seashells Required 3 (and
# Instruments Required 0, as a seashell hunt's settings have). DBG grant adds the seashells without an item-get.
# The sign one tile north: its text (Place:SBMountTamaranch) names the number. The owl in front of the stairs talks
# when Link first stands there; FourteenClear marks that talk as done.
send DBG setflag FourteenClear 1
expect FLAG.FourteenClear.1 5
warp Field 96.75 20.25 24.75
face up
interact
until textready 10
send DBG text SBMountTamaranch
expect said=.*Egg\sopens\swith\s3\ssecret\sseashells\. 5
dismiss 10
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
# Two seashells: the egg stays shut.
send DBG flag Egg_Performance
expect FLAG.Egg_Performance.0 5
# On the stairs below the egg, the third plays the egg's opening, which opens it.
warp Field 98.25 32.25 8.25
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
await 15 event=[1-9]
free 60
send DBG flag Egg_Performance
expect FLAG.Egg_Performance.1 5
shot egg_open.png
# Walking north enters the ending, not the egg.
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Ending 15
# the ending's event walks Link up the stairs
await 10 event=16
shot egg_ending.png
state
