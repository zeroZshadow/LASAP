# A save loaded from the System page counts as a save load (ap_game.cpp, FileLoad): once Link stands in it, the module
# sends RECV with the loaded save's count, so the client resends the items that save lacks. Raising the counter in
# play (setrecv, not saved) stands for items received after the save was written; the Load brings back the save's
# count. The System page's machine is `choice`, the Load screen's `file`. Run after boot_file1 (no client: `game`
# sends the ITEM line).
free 10
check recv=0\s
send DBG setrecv 5
expect RECV\s5 5
await 10 recv=5\s
# Plus opens the Items page, R the System page; Down to Load, A opens the Load screen.
tap 400 when free 3000
await 10 pane=1\snavi=2
tap 80
await 10 pane=2\snavi=2
tap 8000 when choice:KeyWait 3000
tap 1 when choice:KeyWait 3000
until file:KeyWait 10
# A on the file under the cursor asks to confirm; Right to Load, A loads it.
tap 1 when file:KeyWait 3000
until file:Confirmation 10
guard file:ConfirmationCancel
tap 4000 when file:Confirmation 3000
tap 1 when file:Confirmation 3000
loaded 60
# RECV comes once Link stands in the loaded level (settled); expect reads the port since the tap that loads.
expect RECV\s0 5
guard off file:ConfirmationCancel
check recv=0\s
# The item the client resends next is the save's first missing one: applied.
game ITEM 1 SmallKey 1 5 -1 Small Key (Face Shrine)|Finder
until msg:KeyWaiting 15
check recv=1\s
dismiss 10
