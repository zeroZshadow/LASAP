# An item arrives in the Trendy Game (CraneGame), where Link counts as free while the player steers the crane. Its
# item-get waits until Link leaves the shop, then plays as an event.
# Screenshots: crane_1 in the shop with the item waiting, crane_2 the item-get outside.
guard hurt
warp Field 53.25 11.25 139.5
hold 0 0 32767 until level=CraneGame 5
inlevel 20
level CraneGame
free 10
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1 1
expect OK.grant 5
# The item waits while Link is free in the shop.
wait 2
check event=0 paused=0 carry=0 showq=1(\s|$)
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/crane_1.png
# Out of the door; the item-get starts once Link has been free for a moment.
hold 0 0 -32767 until level=Field 10
inlevel 20
await 5 showq=0\+1
# It shows the received item, not the placeholder.
await 3 shown=22(\s|$)
until textready 10
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/crane_2.png
dismiss 20
free 10
