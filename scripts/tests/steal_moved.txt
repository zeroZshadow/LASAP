# Entrances and stealing: the Library's door (Field_12A) leads into the shop and the shop's door (Field_10D) into the
# Library, so the shop's way out leads out of the Library's door, whose arrival entry is not ExitOutShop. Leaving
# with a stolen item still gives it and clears StealSuccess (ap_jumps.cpp, TickStolenGoods), and the shopkeeper lets
# Link back in. Sets the steal flags instead of stealing. Run after boot_file1 on a mod from make_test_patch.py
# --entrances '{"Field_12A": "Shop_01A", "Field_10D": "Library_01A"}'.
guard hurt
send DBG setflag ScoopGet 0
expect FLAG.ScoopGet.0 5
# the Library's door, north of Link, into the shop
warp Field 5.25 10.5 137.5
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Shop 10
inlevel 20
level Shop
free 10
send DBG setflag ShopShovelSteal 1
expect FLAG.ShopShovelSteal.1 5
send DBG setflag StealSuccess 1
expect FLAG.StealSuccess.1 5
# its way out, south of Link, out of the Library's door: the stolen item's get plays there
hold 0 0 -32767 until level=Field 10
loaded 20
at 5.25 136.5 2
dismiss 20
poll FLAG.StealSuccess.0 10 DBG flag StealSuccess
send DBG flag ShopShovelSteal
expect FLAG.ShopShovelSteal.0 5
send DBG flag ScoopGet
expect FLAG.ScoopGet.1 5
free 20
# back in: no punishment, Link stands free in the shop
hold 0 0 32767 until level=Shop 10
inlevel 20
level Shop
free 15
wait 3
free 5
