# Instant Text (run after newgame_file2, on a mod patched with Instant Text on): each page of text shows whole at
# once and still waits for a button. A received item's text, then Marin's lines (A talks to her and turns the pages).
# STATE read= counts the frames the last page spent printing (the message window's Reading state): a few frames
# with Instant Text, dozens letter by letter.
# Screenshots: text_received, text_talk_1 and text_talk_2, each as soon as the page waits for a button.
level MarinTarinHouse
send /game ITEM 1 Rupee20 1 -1 -1 Progressive Mirror Shield|Finder
await 5 showq=(1|0\+1)(\s|$)
until msg:KeyWaiting 10
check read=[0-3](\s|$)
shot text_received.png
# The page stays until a button.
wait 2
check event=[1-9].* showq=0\+1 .*msg=KeyWaiting
dismiss 20
check showq=0
# Marin stands to the right of Link.
face right
interact
until msg:KeyWaiting 5
check read=[0-3](\s|$)
shot text_talk_1.png
wait 2
check event=[1-9].*msg=KeyWaiting
# B turns the page: the module sees it leave KeyWaiting on the frame it does (a page turn takes a few frames).
tapuntil 2 !msg:KeyWaiting when textready 5
until msg:KeyWaiting 5
check read=[0-3](\s|$)
shot text_talk_2.png
dismiss 20
