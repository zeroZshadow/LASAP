# Eagle's Tower's dungeon map dots: run after newgame_file2 on a make_test_patch.py mod with Map Tracker on (18 dots
# in the tower: 1F 5, 2F 7, 3F 2, 4F 2, and 2 in the rooms of its collapsed top). Each floor shows its own dots, each
# in its room's box (inroom=, the box the page places them by: the tower's rooms sit east and south of their names'
# rows and columns); the 3F chests' dots show in their rooms before the collapse, and once the pillars are broken
# (TowerPillarBrokenAll) on 3F, in the collapsed top's rooms, with the 4F rooms. Dot order (ap_map.txt): 02G 03G 08D
# 07A 05A 05D 01B 01C 04A 04B 03D 01G 05G 02H 06H 02D 05D 04B. Screenshots map_d7_1f to map_d7_4f and
# map_d7_collapsed_3f, in the sandbox folder.
warp Lv07EagleTower 22.5 0 114
tapuntil 800 paused=1 when free 5
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect page=1.dots=18.shown=5.inroom=18.*floor=1.*looks=IIiiiiIIIIIIIIIIiI 5
shot map_d7_1f.png
tap 2000 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect dots=18.shown=7.inroom=18.*floor=2.*looks=IIIIIIiiiiiIIIIiIi 5
shot map_d7_2f.png
tap 2000 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect dots=18.shown=2.inroom=18.*floor=3.*looks=IIIIIIIIIIIIiIiIII 5
shot map_d7_3f.png
tap 2000 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect dots=18.shown=2.inroom=18.*floor=4.*looks=iiIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII 5
shot map_d7_4f.png
tapuntil 800 paused=0 when dmap:KeyWait 5
free 10
# The pillars broken: 3F shows the collapsed top (the boss, the instrument and the 3F chests' rooms after the
# collapse), and the 3F rooms from before it no longer.
send DBG setflag TowerPillarBrokenAll 1
expect FLAG.TowerPillarBrokenAll.1 5
tapuntil 800 paused=1 when free 5
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect dots=18.shown=5.inroom=18.*floor=1.*looks=IIiiiiIIIIIIIIIIiI 5
tap 2000 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
until dmap:KeyWait 10
tap 2000 2 2 when dmap:KeyWait
until dmap:KeyWait 10
send DBG mapdot
expect dots=18.shown=4.inroom=18.*floor=3.*looks=iiIIIIIIIIIiIiIIII 5
shot map_d7_collapsed_3f.png
tapuntil 800 paused=0 when dmap:KeyWait 5
free 10
send DBG setflag TowerPillarBrokenAll 0
expect FLAG.TowerPillarBrokenAll.0 5
