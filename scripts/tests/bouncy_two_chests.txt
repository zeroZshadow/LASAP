# Bouncy Items: a chest stays open whatever else is picked up while its ball is out (DESIGN.md, Bouncy Items, Opened when
# taken). Woods North Cave: the chest (woods-north-cave-chest) opened, then the free-standing item taken. The Goponga
# Swamp cave's two chests (goponga-cave-left, 01A; goponga-cave-right, 01B) opened, the left one's ball taken: both
# stay open, and after the level loads anew the taken one is open and the other closed, launching its ball again.
# Run after boot_file1 on a mod with Bouncy Items and Instant Pickups on Everything, these locations not taken.
# The opened flags stand for the chests' looks: the chest closes on an event that finds its flag off.
# Screenshots: two_chests_cave, two_chests_taken, two_chests_again.
send DBG setflag TBOX_OPEN_MysteriousWC02_01A 0
send DBG setflag WoodsNorthCaveHeartGet 0
warp MysteriousWoodsCave02 12.75 0 3.75
face up
interact
poll OK.drop.*spin=[1-9] 8 DBG drop
free 8
send DBG pos 2.25 0.08 3.75
expect OK.pos 2
poll FLAG.WoodsNorthCaveHeartGet.1 6 DBG flag WoodsNorthCaveHeartGet
send DBG flag TBOX_OPEN_MysteriousWC02_01A
expect FLAG.TBOX_OPEN_MysteriousWC02_01A.1 3
shot two_chests_cave.png
send DBG setflag TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01A 0
send DBG setflag TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01B 0
warp Field 138.75 7.5 179.25
warp GopongaCave 11.25 0 3.75
send DBG enemies kill
expect OK 5
face up
interact
poll OK.drop.1[0-9]\. 8 DBG drop
free 8
send DBG drop at 11.25 0.5 6.5
expect OK.drop 2
# The right chest stands behind a skull, lifted from the side.
goto 23.25 1.5 5.25
send DBG enemies kill
expect OK 5
face right
interact
until carry=1+free 3
face left
tap 1
until carry=0 3
goto 24.75 1.5 5.25
face up
interact
poll OK.drop.2[0-9]\. 8 DBG drop
free 8
send DBG drop at 21.75 1 8.25
expect OK.drop 2
send DBG drop launch A10028005CED15A9
expect OK.drop 2
send DBG drop at 24.75 1.9 5.25
expect OK.drop 2
poll FLAG.TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01A.1 10 DBG flag TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01A
free 8
send DBG flag TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01B
expect FLAG.TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01B.1 3
shot two_chests_taken.png
warp Field 138.75 7.5 179.25
warp GopongaCave 23.25 1.5 5.25
send DBG flag TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01A
expect FLAG.TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01A.1 3
send DBG flag TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01B
expect FLAG.TBOX_OPEN_GopongaCave_01B.0 3
send DBG enemies kill
expect OK 5
face right
interact
until carry=1+free 3
face left
tap 1
until carry=0 3
goto 24.75 1.5 5.25
face up
interact
poll OK.drop.2[0-9]\. 8 DBG drop
shot two_chests_again.png
