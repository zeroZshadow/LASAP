# Received Secret Medicines while Link holds one wait in the save (DESIGN.md, Items with special handling in the
# module); DBG grant adds them as received items without their display. Once Link has none the next comes with a
# notice: after the medicine revives him (a quarter heart and an Octorok south of Mabe, as deathlink_medicine), then
# with its bit cleared by DBG poke. Run after newgame_file2, on a mod patched with the APMedicine flags. The inventory
# byte +0xac holds the Flippers (bit 0), the medicine (bit 1) and the keys; a new game has none of them.
guard off dead
free 10
check medicine=0/0\s
send DBG grant SecretMedicine 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
check medicine=1/0\s
send DBG grant SecretMedicine 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
send DBG grant SecretMedicine 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
check medicine=1/2\s
free 30
warp Field 41.25 9.0 147.75
send DBG poke 84 0100
hold 0 0 32767 until health=0 10
await 20 health=[1-9]
until free 20
check paused=0
await 5 medicine=1/1\s
send DBG poke ac 00
expect OK.poke 5
await 5 medicine=1/0\s
send DBG poke ac 00
expect OK.poke 5
wait 2
check medicine=0/0\s
