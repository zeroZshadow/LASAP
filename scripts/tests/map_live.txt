# The open maps follow logic updates (DESIGN.md, Map tracker): run after newgame_file2 on a mod patched with Map
# Tracker on, with the client connected; the client sends the INLOGIC lines given here (/game) and leaves the game
# with that logic. One frame after the last line the open page's icons or dots take their looks and the cursor's name
# its text. DBG map gives the overworld page's markers of ours (made=) and those shown by look (drawn=<in>,<partly>,
# <out>,<collected>) and, last, the name the cursor took (name=); DBG mapdot's looks= are the dots as drawn.
# Screenshots map_live_partly and map_live_dot.
# Overworld: the cursor starts on Mabe Shop's icon (locations 55, 56 and 64) in front of the shop.
warp Field 51.75 10.5 117
tapuntil 800 paused=1 when free 5
until map:KeyWait 10
send DBG map
expect hover=\d+/3/ 5
send /game INLOGIC CLEAR
poll hover=\d+/3/out\smade=\d+\sdrawn=0,0,\d+,\d+\s\|\sname=0/3\sMabe\sShop$ 5 DBG map
send /game INLOGIC 55
poll hover=\d+/3/partly\s.*name=1/3\sMabe\sShop$ 5 DBG map
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/map_live_partly.png
# The same look with another count: the module gives the cursor the new name.
send /game INLOGIC 56
poll hover=\d+/3/partly\s.*name=2/3\sMabe\sShop$ 5 DBG map
send /game INLOGIC 64
poll hover=\d+/3/in\s.*name=3/3\sMabe\sShop$ 5 DBG map
tapuntil 800 paused=0 when map:KeyWait 5
free 10
