# Warp home with BowWow following. Sets what rescuing him sets (companion bit 0 of inventory byte 0x9d, as LASR
# uses it, and BowWowJoin), warps into Tarin's house so he comes along, then warps home with the menu: Link and BowWow
# come out of the door. Clears both at the end, so later scripts walk alone. Screenshots: bowwow_1 in the house,
# bowwow_2 outside.
send DBG setflag BowWowJoin 1
expect FLAG.BowWowJoin.1 5
send DBG poke 9d 01
expect OK.poke 5
warp MarinTarinHouse 6.85 0 3.83
# The screenshot waits for BowWow to come in.
wait 2
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/bowwow_1.png
hold 800 0 0 until !event=0 5
until msg:Selecting 10
choose 0
inlevel 20
level Field
loaded 20
at 38.25 128.5 1.5
# BowWow comes out of the door a few seconds after Link, as when walking out; the screenshot waits for him.
wait 5
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/bowwow_2.png
state
send DBG setflag BowWowJoin 0
expect FLAG.BowWowJoin.0 5
send DBG poke 9d 00
expect OK.poke 5
