# Instant Pickups at the Fairy Queen (DESIGN.md, Instant pickups): her two item-gets (D0-fairy-1, D0-fairy-2) come
# one after the other, and her event waits for their popups, so both show and are gone while Link is still in her room,
# before her goodbye and the whiteout that warps him out. B on each of her pages as soon as it takes one, as a
# hurried player. Run after boot_file1 with config.ini [tweaks] instant_pickups = everything; clears her flags.
# Screenshots: instant_fairy_queen the second popup in her room.
send DBG setflag FairyQueenFirstClear 0
expect FLAG.FairyQueenFirstClear.0 5
# The room's event box (37.5 42) starts her talk: Link walks up into it.
warp Lv10ClothesDungeon 37.5 0 46.5
guard anim=item_get_st
hold 0 0 32767 until !free 5
tapuntil 2 show=1 when textready 15
await 3 popup=1
# None of her pages takes a button until the second popup is gone: her event waits for it.
guard textready
await 5 popup=0
await 3 popup=1
check level=Lv10ClothesDungeon
shot instant_fairy_queen.png
await 5 popup=0
guard off textready
tapuntil 2 level=Field when textready 20
guard off anim=item_get_st
send DBG setflag FairyQueenFirstClear 0
expect FLAG.FairyQueenFirstClear.0 5
send DBG setflag Lv10DungeonClear 0
expect FLAG.Lv10DungeonClear.0 5
