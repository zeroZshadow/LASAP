# DeathLink with Tracy's Secret Medicine, the item the game over revives Link with (a bottled fairy never does), on
# the deathlink.txt setup (after boot_file1 or deathlink), with Link not holding the medicine. DBG give 21 adds it:
# bit 1 of the inventory's byte ac. A received death kills Link past the medicine, which he keeps; then an own death
# (a quarter heart and an Octorok south of Mabe) ends in the medicine's rescue and sends nothing. Ends with Link alive
# and free in Mabe, the medicine used up. Patterns use \s for spaces.
guard off dead
warp Field 38.25 10.5 128.25
send DBG peek i ac 1
expect PEEK\si\sac\s[0-9a-f][014589cd] 5
send DBG give 21 1 0
expect OK\sgive 5
send DBG peek i ac 1
expect PEEK\si\sac\s[0-9a-f][2367abef] 5
# A received death: the game over shows despite the medicine, nothing goes back, and Link keeps the medicine.
peer /die Finder fell past the medicine.
logged DeathLink:\sFinder\sfell\spast\sthe\smedicine 10 client
until health=0 10
until paused=1 15
wait 1
logcount Sending\sdeath 0 client
logcount DeathLink:\sLink 0 peer
send DBG flag APDeathLink
expect FLAG\sAPDeathLink\s0 5
tapuntil 1 paused=0 when paused=1 20
await 30 pos=[1-9]
until free 20
check health=[1-9]
send DBG peek i ac 1
expect PEEK\si\sac\s[0-9a-f][2367abef] 5
# An own death: the medicine refills Link's hearts and he plays on; no game over, no death sent.
warp Field 41.25 9.0 147.75
send DBG poke 84 0100
hold 0 0 32767 until health=0 10
await 20 health=[1-9]
until free 20
check paused=0
send DBG peek i ac 1
expect PEEK\si\sac\s[0-9a-f][014589cd] 5
wait 2
check paused=0
logcount Sending\sdeath 0 client
logcount DeathLink:\sLink 0 peer
warp Field 38.25 10.5 128.25
