# Digs up the item in the owl statue's pen in Ukuku Prairie (above-cave-dig, Field_11I, a buried drop actor at
# 125.25 9.75 128.25) with the Slime Key owned, which would hide the slime key LASR buries there; the item hops out and
# Link walks north into it. Clears the location's got flag. Grants the Slime Key and the
# Shovel and puts the Shovel on Y (sandbox saves only); kills the pen's enemies; Link digs the cell north of him.
# Screenshots: dig_1 after the dig, dig_2 the item-get.
guard hurt
send DBG setflag AboveCaveDigShellGet 0
send DBG grant SlimeKey 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
send DBG grant Shovel 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
send DBG poke a2 07
expect OK.poke 5
warp Field 126.75 9.75 129.75
poll OK.enemies.killed 3 DBG enemies kill
goto 125.25 9.75 129.75
face up
tap 8
free 5
shot dig_1.png
# The location's item may be a trap.
guard off hurt
hold 0 0 32767 until !free 5
until textready 10
state
shot dig_2.png
dismiss 20
poll FLAG.AboveCaveDigShellGet.1 5 DBG flag AboveCaveDigShellGet
