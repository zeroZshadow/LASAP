# The map tracker: run after newgame_file2 on a mod patched with Map Tracker on. In a new game every overworld icon
# shows; the overworld map is revealed and the Tail Cave map shows every room and its chests (screenshots
# map_overworld and map_dungeon). DBG map prints "map=<on> icons=<unchecked>/<total> ... hooks=<dungeon map opens>,
# <icon updates>,<icons of ours>,<chests checked on the server> out=<shown|hidden>". The maps' KeyWait state (the
# map is open and takes buttons) gates the screenshots and the presses.
send DBG map
expect map=1.icons=(\d+)/\1. 5
warp Field 38.25 10.5 128.25
tapuntil 800 paused=1 when free 5
until map:KeyWait 10
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/map_overworld.png
# X toggles the out-of-logic icons instead of opening the memories; the map stays open.
tap 4 2 2 when map:KeyWait
send DBG map
expect out=hidden 5
check paused=1
tap 4 2 2 when map:KeyWait
send DBG map
expect out=shown 5
tapuntil 800 paused=0 when map:KeyWait 5
free 10
warp Lv01TailCave 52.5 0 92.25
tapuntil 800 paused=1 when free 5
until dmap:KeyWait 10
# Opening the dungeon map ran the reveal hooks and updated the added icons.
send DBG map
expect hooks=[1-9]\d*,[1-9]\d*,[1-9] 5
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/map_dungeon.png
tapuntil 800 paused=0 when dmap:KeyWait 5
