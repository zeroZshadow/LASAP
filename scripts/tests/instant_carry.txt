# Instant Pickups while Link holds something over his head, in the Ghost House (GhostHouse_01A). He throws the pot
# that holds ghost-house-pot (12 0 8.25) as ghost_house_pot does, so its item drops where it stood, lifts the pot at
# 10.5 0 9.75 from its left, and walks, carrying it, up into the item. No item-get: the popup shows and he still holds
# the pot (an item-get would take it out of the game). Then an item from the server shows as a popup while he carries
# it, without waiting for his hands to be free. Run after boot_file1 or newgame_file2 (the location unchecked) on a mod
# patched with Instant Pickups. Grants the Power Bracelet (sandbox saves only).
# Screenshots: instant_carry the find's popup over the pot.
guard hurt
send DBG grant PowerBraceletLv1 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
warp Field 99.75 7.5 186.75
at 99.75 186.75
hold 0 0 32767 until level=GhostHouse 5
inlevel 15
free 10
goto 12 0 9.75
at 12 9.75
face up
interact
until carry=1+free 3
tap 1
until carry=0 3
free 5
# Another pot, lifted from its left
goto 9 0 9.75
at 9 9.75
face right
interact
until carry=1+free 3
goto 12 0 9.75
at 12 9.75
check \scarry=1\s
# The location's item may be a trap.
guard off hurt
guard anim=item_get_st
guard msg:Reading
# The pickup's event takes him for a moment: the stick lets go there
hold 0 0 32767 until !free 3
await 3 popup=1
check \scarry=1\s
shot instant_carry.png
await 5 toast=1
send DBG text SystemSaving
expect said=You\sfound 5
await 5 popup=0
await 10 toast=0
# An item from the server while he carries the pot
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1
expect OK.grant 5
await 3 popup=1
check \scarry=1\s
await 5 toast=1
send DBG text SystemSaving
expect said=You\sgot\sSeashell 5
await 5 popup=0
check \scarry=1\s
guard off msg:Reading
guard off anim=item_get_st
