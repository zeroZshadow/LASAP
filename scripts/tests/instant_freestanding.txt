# Instant Pickups with a free-standing item: the heart piece on the grass island among the holes (diamond-island,
# Field_05E, an item actor at 66.75 13.5 54.75), as diamond_island. Link walks up into it from one cell south; its
# popup shows with no item-get, and he is free again while it shows. Run after newgame_file2 (the sandbox's file 1 has
# the location checked) on a mod patched with Instant Pickups.
# Screenshots: instant_freestanding the popup.
guard hurt
warp Field 66.75 13.5 56.25
at 66.75 56.25
guard anim=item_get_st
guard msg:Reading
# The stick lets go once the find is queued, or he walks on into the holes.
hold 0 0 32767 until !free 5
await 3 popup=1
free 5
check popup=1
shot D:/DEV/LASAP-sandbox/instant_freestanding.png
# The heart piece's autosave shows its own popup first; the toast follows it.
await 5 toast=1
send DBG text SystemSaving
expect said=You\sfound 5
await 5 popup=0
guard off msg:Reading
guard off anim=item_get_st
guard off hurt
