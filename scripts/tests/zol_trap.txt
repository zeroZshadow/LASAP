# The Zol Trap lets out Zols (DBG zols: where the last trap let them out, the traps so far, and its enemies alive as
# <actor id>@<x>,<y>,<z>/<zone>; EnemyZolGreen is 1b). Run after newgame_file2 with no LAS client connected: `game`
# sends the ITEM lines as the client does. Two chests hold placeholders and are closed in a new game: the beach chest
# (beach-chest, local id 11, Field_15F, chest at 87.75 6 171.75) and the Armos Maze cave's (armos-cave, local id
# 69, ArmosShrineCave_01A, chest at 6.75 0 5.25). Health is set to 3 hearts before each trap; the Zols may hit Link.
# - Found: the chest's own Zol Trap comes back with the chest's location, and the chest lets out its Zol: in the cave
#   the game's (it jumps out of the chest and lands on the floor), on the overworld one between the chest and Link.
#   Each still stands on the floor 2 s later.
# - Received (another player's, no chest): two Zols one tile left and right of Link, in his zone, on the overworld and
#   in a dungeon room; a level jump takes them away with their room.
# Screenshots: zol_chest_field, zol_chest_cave, zol_field, zol_dungeon.
