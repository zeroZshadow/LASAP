# The Ballad at the Wind Fish's Egg with no instruments, to hear the egg's scene (PlayOrchestra plays
# BGM_ENSEMBLE_INST with every track silent, beside Link's ocarina). Run after newgame_file2 (default starting gear:
# the ocarina). DBG grant adds the Ballad without an item-get; FourteenClear keeps the owl at the stairs from talking.
send DBG setflag FourteenClear 1
expect FLAG.FourteenClear.1 5
send DBG grant Song_WindFish 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 10
# The ocarina (item 0x0c) on X with the Ballad selected (song 1), as the item menu sets them: inventory bytes a3, a1.
send DBG poke a1 01
expect OK.poke 5
send DBG poke a3 0c
expect OK.poke 5
# On the stairs below the egg, inside the area where the Ballad starts the egg's scene.
warp Field 98.25 32.25 8.25
interact 4
until event=22 10
free 90
send DBG setflag FourteenClear 0
expect FLAG.FourteenClear.0 5
state
