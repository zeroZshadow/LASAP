# Instant Pickups on Incoming (config.ini [tweaks] instant_pickups = incoming): a received item shows as a popup, its
# toast names it and no event starts; Link's own find plays the game's item-get with its text, also while that popup
# still shows, and queues no popup of its own. Opens beach-chest as beach_chest does. Run after boot_file1 on a save
# where the chest is closed.
# Screenshots: incoming_received the popup, incoming_find the item-get.
guard hurt
send DBG grant PowerBraceletLv1 1 -1 0
expect OK.grant 5
warp Field 86.25 6 173.25
at 86.25 173.25
face right
interact
until carry=1+free 3
face left
tap 1
until carry=0 3
goto 87.75 6 173.25
at 87.75 173.25
face up
guard event=22
send DBG grant Seashell 1 -1
expect OK.grant 5
await 3 popup=1
await 3 shown=22(\s|$)
send DBG text SystemSaving
expect said=You\sgot\sSeashell 5
shot incoming_received.png
guard off event=22
# The location's item may be a trap.
guard off hurt
check popup=1
interact
until textready 10
shot incoming_find.png
dismiss 20
await 5 popup=0
until free 10
check showq=0(\s|$)
