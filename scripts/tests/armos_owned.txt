# Boss drops (DESIGN.md, Drop actor) with the boss's item owned before the fight: the Face Key received from
# elsewhere, Link beats the Armos Knight (ArmosShrine_02A) and its drop still pops and can be taken. Run after
# boot_file1; resets the boss's and the drop's flags and takes the Face Key away again.
# Screenshots: armos_owned_pop.
guard off hurt
send DBG setflag FaceKeyPop_Btl_ArmosShrine_02A 0
send DBG setflag APBossDrop_armos-knight 0
send DBG setflag APDropGet_armos-knight 0
send DBG grant FaceKey 1 5
expect OK.grant 5
warp ArmosShrine 7.5 1.5 32.25
hold 0 0 32767 until !free 10
until free 60
poll ERR.hit.event 30 DBG hit EnemyDeguArmos
poll FLAG.APBossDrop_armos-knight.1 30 DBG flag APBossDrop_armos-knight
poll OK.drop.-?[0-9] 8 DBG drop
shot armos_owned_pop.png
droprest 20
gotodrop 5
until textready 10
dismiss 20
poll FLAG.APDropGet_armos-knight.1 5 DBG flag APDropGet_armos-knight
send DBG grant FaceKey 0 5
expect OK.grant 5
