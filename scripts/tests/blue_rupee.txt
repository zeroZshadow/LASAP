# Blue Rupees off (DESIGN.md, optional locations; Drop actor, The blue rupees): the Color Dungeon's blue rupees are
# checks, optional ones, each a drop actor where it stands. Link, a tile above rupee 1 (D0-rupee-1,
# Lv10ClothesDungeon_08D at 49.5 0.62 87.75), walks down into it: it shows the location's item and sets its flag (the
# rupee's own, Lv10RupeeGet), and it is gone, also once the level loads again (DBG actor). Run after newgame_file2 on a mod patched with Blue Rupees off; clears the rupee's flag.
# Screenshot: blue_rupee.
guard hurt
send DBG setflag Lv10RupeeGet 0
expect FLAG.Lv10RupeeGet.0 5
warp Lv10ClothesDungeon 49.5 0 86.25
poll OK.actor.1F01C1005D036766.1 5 DBG actor 1F01C1005D036766
# The location's item may be a trap.
guard off hurt
hold 0 0 -32767 until !free 5
until textready 10
state
shot blue_rupee.png
dismiss 20
poll FLAG.Lv10RupeeGet.1 5 DBG flag Lv10RupeeGet
send DBG actor 1F01C1005D036766
expect OK.actor.1F01C1005D036766.0 5
