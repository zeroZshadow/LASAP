# Kiki and BowWow: with BowWow following, Kiki picks no fight (Kiki.bfevfl's Bowwow_talk, patches.hpp kRandoFixes),
# and trades the bananas for his check. Makes BowWow follow and sets TradeBananasGet instead of the trade quest. Run
# after newgame_file2 on a mod without Completed Bridge; clears StickDrop, which keeps Kiki away
# (ap_option_edits.npc_rows); leaves BowWow following and the bridge built.
send DBG setflag StickDrop 0
expect FLAG.StickDrop.0 5
send DBG poke 9d 01
expect OK 5
send DBG setflag TradeBananasGet 1
expect FLAG.TradeBananasGet.1 5
warp Field 171.75 7.5 97.5
send DBG peek i 9d 1
expect PEEK.i.9d.01 5
# beside Kiki with BowWow, where the fight starts in the game
guard event=22
goto 171.75 7.5 93.5
wait 5
guard off event=22
goto 171.75 7.5 92.75
face up
interact 1 10
advance 2 30
choose 0
advance 2 120
free 30
send DBG flag StickDrop
expect FLAG.StickDrop.1 5
# the item-get's text named the kiki location (ids.json 90)
send DBG text
expect found=90\s 5
